Privacy Policy (Mock)

CauniX values your privacy. We collect only essential data: Google account email (for identity), call metadata (duration, timestamp) — but never call audio content. End-to-end encryption ensures only participants can access conversations.

No voice recordings, no third-party sharing, no phone numbers stored. You control your CauniX ID and can delete your data anytime.
